How it works

An AI system that pressure-tests a decision against history before you commit to it.

Cornerstone Labs runs a proposed decision or strategy through Retrodictive Validation™: pattern-matching it against hundreds of comparable situations across history to find where the reasoning is thin and what would have to be true for it to work.

The mechanism

Pattern-matching against history. The same claim has usually been tested before, somewhere.

The output

A stress-tested report with a paper trail, built to be defended in front of stakeholders actively looking for the weak point.

Who it's for

Built for the person who has to answer for it when the decision goes wrong.

Julian Vance has been watching this specific failure for thirty years.

The pattern repeats across every organization he's been close to. Someone champions an idea early. Others stay quiet because challenging it costs more than staying agreeable. The analysis gets built to support a conclusion that was reached months before anyone called it a decision.
